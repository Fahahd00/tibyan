# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Tibyan serves people seeking reliable religious knowledge. We hold ourselves to the same
standards of honesty, respect, and care that the project asks of its sources.

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of
background, experience level, or role on the team.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in reviews, issues, and discussions.
- Critique code and decisions, never people.
- Be honest about what works and what does not — never overstate test results or demo capabilities.
- Respect scholarly sources: never fabricate, alter, or misattribute religious content.
- Respect user privacy: never copy real user questions or personal data into issues, logs, or tests.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Deliberately introducing fabricated sources or citations.
- Bypassing safety review for changes that affect sources, classification, or the safety gate.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the team lead or product lead. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, revert commits, or restrict access for
behaviour that violates this code.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
